Jarvis is a privately operated personal AI assistant for email, research and market briefings, with optional delivery to Slack. It runs from the operator's computer using a local Hermes agent and a configured AI provider.

01 / Email

Read. Understand. Draft.

Search authorized Gmail accounts, summarize selected conversations and prepare replies. Sending an email requires two separate confirmations in Jarvis.

02 / Research

Context with sources.

Review public research and dated market briefings. Sources and delayed observations remain visible so you can assess what is current.

03 / Delivery

Review before sharing.

Prepare selected content for a chosen Slack conversation. Check the destination, message and files, then confirm before posting.

Google account access, explained.

Jarvis requests Gmail read access for inbox assistance and Gmail send access for messages you explicitly approve. It does not request permission to delete, archive or relabel your mail.

AI-assisted requests can send prompts and relevant retrieved email content to the configured inference provider. Local operation does not mean that all processing stays on your device.

Read the privacy policy for details on permissions, external processing, retention and disconnecting an account.
