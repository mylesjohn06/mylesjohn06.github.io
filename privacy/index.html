Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026 · Operator: mylesjohn06

External AI processing. Nous Privacy Mode was enabled for the operator's personal account on October 19, 2026. It covers Nous's handling of inference payloads, subject to its policy; downstream providers have their own policies.

1. Scope and operator

This policy describes the current privately operated Jarvis assistant and this informational website. The operator is mylesjohn06. Jarvis is independent of Google, Slack and its AI providers. The public website does not offer access to the private assistant or initiate an OAuth connection.

2. Information accessed

When you authorize Gmail and request a feature, Jarvis can access your account address, message identifiers, senders, recipients, subjects, dates, snippets and message text needed for the request. Its current Gmail reader lists attachment names but does not download their contents. Jarvis also processes prompts, responses, voice transcripts, selected uploads, message drafts and delivery records.

3. Google permissions and purposes

gmail.readonly enables requested inbox search and reading. gmail.send enables sending messages that you explicitly approve. No Gmail modify/delete scope is requested. Email sending requires two confirmations. Sharing selected email content to Slack requires its own review and confirmation; reading an email does not itself authorize sharing it.

4. AI processing and other disclosures

Hermes runs locally but calls the configured AI inference provider. Relevant retrieved email content, prompts, conversation context and responses can leave the computer for AI processing. The current default integration is Nous Portal, which may route requests to other model or infrastructure providers. Provider retention and training practices depend on applicable terms and account settings. Nous Privacy Mode is enabled for the operator's personal account as of October 19, 2026. Its policy describes limits on retaining and using inference payloads for training, product improvement and support, with operational, security and legal exceptions. This control does not change downstream providers' data practices. See the Nous privacy policy and terms. Jarvis does not itself train AI models using Gmail content. Google processes authorized Gmail API operations. Slack receives only confirmed messages and selected files, under Slack's own retention/access policies. Public web searches disclose the submitted query to the configured search service. Browser speech recognition may process microphone audio through browser or operating-system services under their own terms.

5. Google data and Limited Use

Google-derived data is intended only for the user-facing email and productivity features requested by the account holder, not for advertising, sale, credit decisions or general-purpose AI training. Any use and transfer must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use. Before claiming readiness for broader Google-account use, the operator must verify that AI-provider settings and applicable third-party terms meet these restrictions. This website does not certify third-party compliance or claim that Jarvis has completed Google verification.

6. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored in local account files or operator-managed environment settings. Jarvis does not ask for your Google password. Hermes may retain conversations, tool results, responses and memories locally; no universal automatic deletion deadline is configured. Unsent email drafts live in memory and expire after 24 hours or a server restart. Slack drafts and receipts use a local SQLite store; drafts expire after one hour. Staged files and final payloads older than 24 hours are cleaned up on later store access, not by a guaranteed background purge; receipts may remain. Logs can contain account identifiers, search queries, tool names, timings and errors. Backups persist until the operator removes them.

7. Security, disconnecting and deletion

The assistant uses loopback hosting by default, OAuth for Gmail and server-side outbound approval controls. Local data is not all encrypted by Jarvis; protect the computer and backups. You can revoke access using Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not delete existing local copies, backups or third-party records. Request access, correction or deletion from the operator at mylesjohn06@gmail.com; do not send passwords or tokens. Third-party deletion requests and legal retention obligations are handled separately.

8. Public website and changes

This informational site has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, external fonts or application sign-in. The host may process IP addresses and request logs. If hosted on GitHub Pages, the GitHub privacy statement applies to its hosting operations. Update this page when data practices change and obtain any required consent.
